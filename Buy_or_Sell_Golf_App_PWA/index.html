Official Rule Book

1. Before You Start

Designed for a foursome. Each player starts with a $40 bankroll. No player can lose more than $40.

2. Holes 1–3: The IPO

Everyone plays normal stroke play. After Hole 3, the player in last place becomes the first stock on the market. Opening price is $1.

3. Dynamic Handicap

After holes 3, 6, 9, 12, and 15, update each player's bonus:

  • 1st place: 0 strokes
  • 2nd place: 1 stroke
  • 3rd place: 2 strokes
  • 4th place: 3 strokes

Bonus strokes apply only when that golfer is part of a Best Ball team.

4. Buying Stock

The purchased golfer becomes your partner. Your side plays Best Ball against the remaining players.

5. Dividends

When your team wins a hole, each opposing player pays your team $2. Ties pay nothing.

6. Trading Windows

After holes 6, 9, 12, and 15, the market opens. Any player may offer to buy the owned golfer. Each offer must double the previous price: $1 → $2 → $4 → $8 → $16 max.

7. Final Three: Take Private

At the start of Hole 16, the player in last place may buy themselves back at the current stock price, up to $16. They play solo against everyone for holes 16–18. If they win, they collect the remaining pot, subject to the $40 cap.

Quick Start

  1. Play holes 1–3 normally.
  2. Last place becomes the IPO at $1.
  3. Buyer gets that player as a Best Ball partner.
  4. Every winning hole pays a $2 dividend from each opposing player.
  5. After holes 6, 9, 12, and 15, open the market.
  6. Buyouts must double: $1, $2, $4, $8, $16 max.
  7. Update dynamic handicap every 3 holes.
  8. On hole 16, last place can “take private” and play solo for the pot.
